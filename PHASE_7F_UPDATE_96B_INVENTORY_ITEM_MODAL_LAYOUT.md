# Update 96B - Inventory Item Modal Layout

- Reorganized Item Identity, Classification, and Pricing & Inventory sections.
- Status moved to the upper-right position in the Item Identity row.
- Classification uses Stock Type, Category, Sellable followed by UOM mapping rows.
- Purchase UOM Cost now displays its UOM suffix inside the field.
- Removed verbose purchase/basic cost helper copy.
- Current Weighted Average Cost is aligned with Purchase and Basic UOM costs.
- Non-sellable items hide Selling UOM, Selling Conversion, Selling Price, Selling Price Effectivity Date, and Commission Eligibility.
- Reorder Point remains available for tracked Inventory items even when not sellable.
- Supplier Masterlist embedded Create New Inventory Item form is kept aligned with the Inventory Masterlist form.
